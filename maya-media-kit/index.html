About the channel

Maya makes a daily vlog about money and life on the road. She runs the trading academy Yatanium, so the channel mixes personal finance with travel: what things cost, how she pays, how she invests while moving between countries. She travels and films with Pepi, and a small team of editors cuts every video.

They split their time between homes in Spain and Turkey and the road. This autumn it was Mexico, Argentina, Uruguay, Brazil and Paraguay. India is next, in February 2027.
